name: Domain purchase experience
description: Customer requirements for domain onboarding and repeat visits
---
Support both Afro AI purchases and domains bought elsewhere. Provide proper working paths to buy domains and link customer apps, not links going nowhere. Once a client buys, do not bring the purchase over and over again.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested both origins, working buy/link paths, and no repeated purchase prompts after buying.

**How to apply:** Prioritize My Domains for existing customers and prevent duplicate orders for domains they already purchased. In-progress or uncertain registration should lead to status or support, not another payment.
