In Africa, gaming preferences vary significantly between mobile and computer platforms, largely driven by hardware accessibility and data costs. [1, 2] 
Mobile Devices: Casual & Competitive
Mobile gaming is the dominant platform in Africa, where casual games lead in total downloads due to their simple mechanics and low data requirements. [1, 2] 

* Casual & Puzzle: These are the most popular by volume, accounting for 48% of all downloads. Top titles include [Candy Crush Saga](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0rytj3p&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?), [Gardenscapes](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0kyr9z4&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?), and [Homescapes](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11gd67nzxk&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?).
* Action & Shooters: This category generates the most intense engagement and competitive play. Leading titles include [Call of Duty: Mobile](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11h1m5lgf7&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?), [PUBG Mobile](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11dxbkyrq3&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?), and [Free Fire](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11hd5fc5tr&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?).
* Sports: Football (soccer) is a "second language" across the continent, making titles like [EA SPORTS FC Mobile](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11byzdydwl&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?) (formerly FIFA) and [Dream League Soccer](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0120wwh_&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?) perennial top-charters.
* Racing: Popular because many titles offer robust offline modes, which is crucial in areas with expensive or unstable data. [Asphalt 9](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11ggsn_tkm&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?) and [Real Racing](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/065z330&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?) are major hits.
* Hyper-Casual: Fast-growing in regions with limited internet, as these "lite" games (e.g., [Subway Surfers](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0kyvw1z&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?), [Temple Run](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0hrg8ry&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?)) require minimal storage and data to play. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6] 

Computers (PC): Strategy & Core Gaming
PC gaming in Africa typically appeals to a more "core" demographic, focusing on genres that benefit from deeper complexity and better hardware. [5, 7] 

* Action-Adventure: This is the leading genre for the broader African market, commanding roughly 33% of the market share in 2025.
* Strategy & Simulation: PC is the preferred platform for long-term strategy games like [Football Manager](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/02cvrk&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?), which has a massive following in Nigeria and South Africa.
* Role-Playing Games (RPGs): Open-world and narrative-driven RPGs are significantly more popular on computers than on other platforms, as players seek immersive experiences.
* MOBAs: Games like [League of Legends](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11j0q35sms&q=What+types+do+they+like+most++on+mobile+devices+and+computers+?) have a strong and growing competitive scene, particularly among players aged 15–25 in Egypt and Nigeria.
* E-sports: PC remains the primary platform for organized e-sports leagues, which are currently seeing a surge in investment across the continent. [5, 6, 7, 8, 9]
