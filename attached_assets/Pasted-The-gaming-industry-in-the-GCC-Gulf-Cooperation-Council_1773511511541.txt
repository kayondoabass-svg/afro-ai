The gaming industry in the GCC (Gulf Cooperation Council) is a rapidly expanding global powerhouse, valued at approximately $2.24 billion to $2.52 billion in 2024. Driven by high disposable income and state-led investments like Saudi Arabia's [Vision 2030](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/saudi-arabia/control,-alt,-expand-the-rise-and-rise-of-esports-in-the-gcc), the region is a global leader in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU). [1, 2, 3, 4] 
Market Scale & Demographics

* Total Revenue: The combined market of [Saudi Arabia](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/01z215&q=How+about+GCC+countries?), the [UAE](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0j1z8&q=How+about+GCC+countries?), and [Egypt](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/02k54&q=How+about+GCC+countries?) reached roughly $2.0 billion to $2.52 billion in 2024 and is projected to hit $2.8 billion by 2029.
* Player Base: There are over 33.7 million gamers in the GCC as of late 2024, with expectations to reach 38.9 million by 2028.
* High Spending: The [UAE](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0j1z8&q=How+about+GCC+countries?) has one of the world's highest ARPUs at $90 per gamer, surpassing both [Saudi Arabia](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/01z215&q=How+about+GCC+countries?) ($60) and mature markets like [South Korea](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/06qd3&q=How+about+GCC+countries?) ($30). [2, 3, 5, 6, 7] 

Preferred Genres & Platforms
The region favors high-octane, competitive, and social genres. [2, 8] 

| Platform [2, 9, 10, 11, 12] | Most Popular Genres | Key Titles |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | Battle Royale, Sports, Hyper-Casual | PUBG Mobile, Free Fire, EA SPORTS FC Mobile, Subway Surfers |
| PC/Laptop | Action-Adventure, Shooters (FPS), Strategy | Call of Duty, Fortnite[](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0k9bc90&q=How+about+GCC+countries?), Valorant, League of Legends |
| Overall | Sports (57.9%), Fighting (53.8%), Action (53%) | FIFA/FC series, Tekken, Mortal Kombat |

Dominant Regional Players
[Saudi Arabia](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/01z215&q=How+about+GCC+countries?): The largest market, accounting for 49.5% to 59% of regional revenue. Through the [Savvy Games Group](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gAjWuuinXZ4), the government has a $38 billion fund to establish a global gaming empire.
[United Arab Emirates (UAE)](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0j1z8&q=How+about+GCC+countries?): The most "sophisticated" hub with 75% of residents actively gaming. Major projects include Abu Dhabi's $1 billion "Esports Island".
[Kuwait](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/047yc&q=How+about+GCC+countries?) & [Qatar](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0697s&q=How+about+GCC+countries?): Growing interest in high-stakes e-sports; [Qatar](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/m/0697s&q=How+about+GCC+countries?) recently launched a $2.6 million Dota 2 league.
