---
name: Customer identity and branding
description: User rule separating client brands from Afro AI and KEYO vendor identities
---

Never use Afro AI or KEYO as names for client apps or businesses, or invent Afro-prefixed client brands such as AfroBalance. Ask for the customer's preferred name when a new build has no supplied brand; do not silently invent one.

**Why:** The user repeated that this had already been requested and showed a client app labeled AfroBalance. Earlier prompt instructions both prohibited vendor identity and required assuming unknown names without asking.

**How to apply:** Keep customer identity rules unconditional, separate from profile/founder context. Check build prompts, project-agent instructions and publish validation together. Platform identity in product explanations is not customer branding. Existing published apps need an explicit edit and republish; changing generation policy alone does not redesign them.
