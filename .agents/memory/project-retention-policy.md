---
name: Requested project retention policy
description: Founder-requested hidden deletion and long-term retention; legal scope must be established
---

The founder requests that client-deleted projects disappear from the client's
view while their records are retained, mentioning six years or permanent storage.
This is a requested product policy, not confirmation of a universal legal duty.

**Why:** The user asked for retained records after client deletion and stated
that they believe long-term retention is required by law.

**How to apply:** Establish applicable jurisdictions, record categories, lawful
basis, disclosure, access restrictions and retention periods before implementing
an archive. Distinguish archival/recovery from legal holds and permanent erasure.
Do not silently retain every category of personal data indefinitely or claim
that an existing backup provides a legally compliant six-year archive.
