---
name: Upload-processing permission
description: Desired signup disclosures for routine customer-requested processing, without repeated permission interruptions
---

The user wants signup acceptance of Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy
to address customers' rights to upload material and Afro AI's processing of it
to generate their requested outputs, rather than repeatedly pausing ordinary
builds for permission.

**Why:** The user considers repeated permission interruptions unfair to customers.

**How to apply:** Treat this as the desired product workflow, not a conclusion
that signup constitutes blanket legal consent. Clearly disclose necessary AI
provider processing, record affirmative acceptance of policy versions, and
distinguish requested processing from unrelated reuse, training, publication,
new charges and technical provider authorization.
