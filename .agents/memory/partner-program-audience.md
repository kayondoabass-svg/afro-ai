name: Partner programme audience
description: User-defined distinction between individual affiliates and registered business partners, including business teams.
---

The user distinguishes affiliate marketing for an individual from the partner
programme for a registered business entity. A business applicant may represent
a team rather than a single person.

**Why:** The user explicitly set these two routes when inviting prospective
partners and asked for business-team access in the applicant dashboard plan.

**How to apply:** Keep the two application and onboarding paths clear. Plan
business workspace access around an accountable owner and separately authorised
team members, not shared credentials or Afro AI internal-team membership.

Every successful affiliate or business-partner application must email the
founder so they can review it. This is separate from opt-in operational alerts.

Ask applicants whether they prefer bank or mobile money. Payouts are made
manually by the founder; the platform records them but must not initiate money
transfers.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested application notifications and manual
payments to reduce errors and misuse, including if the platform is compromised.

**How to apply:** Never add automatic disbursements to these programmes.
Do not collect banking passwords, PINs or OTPs; require explicit confirmation
that an offline payment has already happened before recording it as paid.
