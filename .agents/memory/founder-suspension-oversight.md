---
name: Founder suspension oversight
description: User requirements for founder visibility and owner suspension notifications.
---
The founder requires working app Details access, visibility into why an app was suspended, and an email alert explaining the suspension to the affected owner.

**Why:** The user could see a suspended customer USSD-related website but could not open its details or determine the reason.

**How to apply:** Expand dedicated founder oversight without widening ordinary customer access to other owners' projects. Distinguish website publication status from USSD service/subscription status. Do not infer a historical suspension reason when the recorded evidence is missing.

Expand the existing infrastructure rather than deleting or replacing it. After the one-month public-hosting period, take the app offline but keep the saved app/project. Preserve limited daily free usage under the existing infrastructure.

**Why:** The user explicitly said, “Dont delete,” and requested that expired apps remain saved with limited usage every 24 hours.

**How to apply:** Make platform repairs additive: preserve existing apps, projects and assets rather than bulk-deleting or resetting them. Keep hosting expiry separate from saved-work access and daily allowance resets. Do not lift unrelated restrictions or turn this requirement into an indefinite-retention promise for every category of customer data.
